# Copywriter Strategy and Delivery

## Diagnose

Establish primary pain, desired outcome, main objection, awareness level, sophistication, honest positioning gap, traffic promise, and one conversion action.

For sales pages/VSLs, load `one-belief.md` and form:

> Doing **[new opportunity]** is the key to **[primary benefit]**, made possible by **[unique mechanism]**.

If no truthful mechanism exists, ask for the missing causal explanation; do not manufacture one.

## Choose one structure

- **Five Acts** for full marketing/sales copy: Hero → Authority/Story → Mechanism → Offer → Close. Load `five-acts.md`.
- **Modified SaaS:** Hero → proof → three-step operation → benefits → audience fit → pricing → FAQ → CTA.
- **Narrative Triad** when founder/identity transformation carries the proof: background (30%) → emotional identification (40%) → discovery (30%). This contract is inline; no external file is required.
- **Compact KSTK** for short sophisticated copy: hook/context → tension → revelation/mechanism → action. Choose narrative or evidence-led form. This contract is inline; no external file is required.
- VSL follows `vsl-craft.md`.

Do not mix structures. Load patterns, offer, fascinations, or anti-pattern references only at their relevant step.

## Voice

Project tone or a brand-voice genome governs. Default is conversational. Keep one voice:

- professional: precise, formal, proof-led
- conversational: direct, relatable, first/second person
- bold: short, provocative, outcome-led
- educational: explain before claiming
- urgent: only from a real constraint

Optional inline voice leans replace the formerly external LightCopy dependency: surprising narrative, strategic authenticity, perceptive observer, or indirect commands. Pick one, never blend all four.

Match the traffic source's promise and tone. When no ad context exists, record that future ads must align with the hero rather than pretending congruence was verified.

## Write and validate

Benefits lead; mechanisms make promises believable; features support; proof is specific and sourced; objections appear in audience language. Sales offers, when applicable, cover anchoring, component stack, purposeful bonuses, honest reason-why, and guarantee.

Before saving:

- remove generic/filler headlines, feature-first framing, fake urgency/proof, text walls, self-centered language, placeholders, competing CTAs, abstract benefits, and unresolved top objections
- verify the central belief and mechanism across the piece
- verify traffic congruence and selected-genome prohibitions/checklist
- ensure every deliverable is ready for direct use

## Delivery

Use the paths in `modes-and-outputs.md`. Include concise frontmatter or a header noting audience evidence, structure, central belief, voice/genome, research status, and congruence status. Do not dump internal chain-of-thought.

Completion summary lists artifacts actually written, sections, validation, primary CTA, and recommended next owner (Refiner, Dev, or QA). Never list hypothetical artifacts as written.
